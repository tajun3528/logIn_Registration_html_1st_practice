About IUBAT

IUBAT (International University of Business Agriculture and Technology) is Bangladesh’s first non‑government university, founded in 1991 by Prof. Dr. M. Alimullah Miyan, with a mission to produce at least one professional graduate from every village and ward of the country. It emphasizes human resource development, scholarships for meritorious students, and a vision of nation‑building through education.



🏫 About IUBAT University 📌 Key Facts Founded: 1991 (initiated in 1989, registered in 1993)
Founder: Prof. Dr. M. Alimullah Miyan
Type: First non‑government university in Bangladesh
Campus: Permanent campus on 6.5 acres of land in Uttara, Dhaka
Students: Over 8,397 enrolled
Faculty: 321 teachers, 1,326 publications
Transport: 35+ buses provide free transportation for students

🌍 Vision & Mission Vision: To produce at least one professional graduate from each village/ward of Bangladesh under the KBAD (Knowledge Based Area Development) program.
Mission: Human resource development through quality education, training, and research.
Scholarships: Available for both new and continuing students, ensuring financial support for meritorious but needy learners.

📚 Academics Offers undergraduate and graduate programs across multiple disciplines:
College of Agricultural Sciences (CAS)
College of Engineering & Technology (Civil, Mechanical, Computer, Electrical, Electronics)
College of Nursing
College of Arts & Sciences
College of Tourism & Hospitality Management
Medical College (planned)

🏆 Achievements Ranked 109th in WURI 2024 Global Top 300 Innovative Universities.
Ranked #1411 in QS World University Rankings 2024.
Recognized in UI Green Metric 2022 for sustainability.

🌿 Campus Life Green, eco‑friendly campus with scenic spots like Bokul Tola Study Arena, Shapla Dighi, and River View. Facilities include laboratories, research centers, cafeterias, mosque, Shaheed Minar, and open auditorium.
