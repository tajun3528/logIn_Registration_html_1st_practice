About Us

IUBAT (International University of Business Agriculture and Technology) is Bangladesh’s first non‑government university, founded in 1991 by Prof. Dr. M. Alimullah Miyan, with a mission to produce at least one professional graduate from every village and ward of the country. It emphasizes human resource development, scholarships for meritorious students, and a vision of nation‑building through education. more
